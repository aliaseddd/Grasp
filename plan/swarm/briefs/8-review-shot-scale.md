# 8-review-shot-scale: `scale.png` against its list

## The goal, and why

A screenshot of a web page is checked by someone who has seen nothing but the image and a list of what it must show, so that what they report is what the image shows and not what they expect. You are that reviewer.

## Read

One image, `canvas/app/shots/scale.png`, and the list below. Open nothing else: no code, no notes, no other image.

## The files you own

None. You change nothing.

## What to do

Look at the image at its full size (it is 2,800 by 1,800 px: 1,400 by 900 at twice the scale). For each item of the list, say **present** or **absent**, and in one sentence what in the image makes you say so. Say "absent" when you cannot see it; do not give the benefit of the doubt.

1. The tab in front is titled `definitions : GeneralView`, with two more tabs beside it.
2. The whole view is on the plane: a grid of several hundred small boxes with bare ground around it on every side, none cut by the plane's edge.
3. Every box is an outline only: no word can be read anywhere on the plane.
4. No line joins any two boxes.
5. The ground has no dots.
6. A minimap at the bottom right shows the same grid as grey rectangles; zoom buttons stand at the bottom left.
7. One bar of 40 px across the top, and a strip at each side of the window; the bar and the strips do not cover the plane.

## What to send back

The seven answers, in the list's order, each "present" or "absent" with its sentence. Then anything else in the image that looks broken, cut off or out of place, in a few lines.

## The rules

You change no file, add no package, and write no test: you look at one image and answer.
